Advertising preferences

With your permission, this site uses Meta Pixel and the Conversions API to measure page visits, campaign views, donation checkouts and successful payments.

Measurement can include your IP address, browser user agent, Meta click and browser identifiers, a pseudonymous visitor identifier, event time, page path, currency and donation amount. Campaign descriptions, medical stories, form contents, card details and donor names are not included in the event data configured by this site. Meta Pixel also receives standard browser request information, such as the page URL and referrer.

Checkout attribution is stored privately for up to 30 days and is removed after successful delivery of the payment event. A receipt without visitor information is retained for up to 30 days to prevent repeated processing. Meta and Stripe apply their own retention policies.

You can accept or reject measurement, and reopen Advertising preferences in the footer to change your choice. A browser Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal disables advertising measurement. Rejecting does not prevent donating.

Cookies used after acceptance: _fbp, _fbc when a Meta ad click identifier is present, and lm_visitor, each for up to 90 days. The advertising preference is remembered for up to 180 days.
